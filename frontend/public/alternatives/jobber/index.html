What Jobber is for

Jobber is field service management software. Providers use it to schedule their own jobs, send quotes, invoice clients, and track work for the customers they already have.

What Luminexa is for

Luminexa is a local service marketplace. Customers search by address, see providers whose service area covers them, and book real availability — snow removal, car detailing, cleaning, and more in Ottawa and Toronto.

When Luminexa is the better fit

Choose Luminexa when you want customers to discover you (or you want to hire help nearby) without cold-calling. Providers publish services, a radius, and a calendar. Customers only see you if they are inside both their search radius and your service area.

When Jobber may still be right

Keep Jobber (or similar FSM tools) if you mainly need back-office software for an existing book of business — quoting, invoicing, and crew dispatch — and you are not looking for a consumer marketplace.

Is Luminexa a Jobber competitor?

Only partly. People searching for a Jobber alternative sometimes want simpler booking or more customer demand. Luminexa competes on marketplace booking; Jobber competes as contractor operations software.

Can I use Luminexa instead of Jobber?

If your goal is getting booked by nearby customers in Ottawa or Toronto, yes — list on Luminexa. If you need full invoicing and CRM for offline clients, you may still want dedicated FSM software alongside or instead.

Where does Luminexa work?

Launch cities are Ottawa and Toronto, Ontario. Search is distance-based: a provider appears only when you are inside both your search radius and their service area.
