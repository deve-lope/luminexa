What Jobber is for

Jobber is field service management software. Providers use it to schedule jobs, send quotes, invoice clients, and track work — especially useful once you have an established book of business and crew workflows.

What Luminexa is for

Luminexa is a booking and business app for local service providers. You publish services (fixed, range, or quote), set availability, share a My page so clients can book open times, message about jobs, manage clients, and send invoices.

When Luminexa is the better value

Choose Luminexa when you are a new or solo provider who wants booking, messaging, and invoices without Jobber-level subscription fees. One Pro plan after the free trial covers the provider dashboard.

When Jobber may still be right

Keep Jobber if you mainly need deep field-service ops — advanced dispatch, large-crew workflows, and back-office built for a busy existing book of business.

Is Luminexa a cheaper Jobber alternative?

For new providers who need booking, clients, and invoices at a low CAD price, yes — Luminexa Pro is $9.99 CAD per month after a free trial. Jobber is a deeper FSM product and typically costs more in USD. They are not identical products.

Can I use Luminexa instead of Jobber?

If you need a simple way for clients to book open times and for you to invoice, yes. If you need full crew dispatch and heavy FSM, you may still want Jobber alongside or instead.

Where is Luminexa available?

The app is available on web, Google Play, and the App Store. Launch focus includes Ottawa and Toronto, Ontario.
