What Square Appointments is for

Square Appointments is scheduling software tied to Square payments. It shines when you want a calendar inside the Square POS and payments ecosystem.

What Luminexa is for

Luminexa is a booking and business app for local services: services catalog, open slots, booking rules, messages, clients, invoices, and a shareable My page — without requiring Square hardware.

When Luminexa is the better fit

Choose Luminexa when you want a dedicated provider dashboard for services, booking, messaging, and invoices at a simple CAD price — especially snow, detailing, cleaning, or handyman-style work.

When Square may still be right

Keep Square if you mainly need POS hardware, gift cards, and a free calendar tightly tied to Square payments.

Is Luminexa free like Square Appointments?

Customer accounts are free. Providers use Luminexa Pro at $9.99 CAD/month after a 30-day free trial. Square offers a free Appointments tier for a single location; paid Square tiers add more features.

Does Luminexa replace Square POS?

No. Luminexa is not a full POS or retail suite. You can keep Square for in-person payments while using Luminexa for booking and job tools.

Where is Luminexa available?

Web, Google Play, and the App Store.
