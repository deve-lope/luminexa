What Housecall Pro is for

Housecall Pro is field-service software for home-service businesses: scheduling, estimates, invoicing, payments, and team tools as you grow.

What Luminexa is for

Luminexa gives providers a dashboard for services, availability, messages, clients, and invoices — plus a shareable My page so clients can book open times. Built for simplicity and a low CAD subscription.

When Luminexa is the better value

Pick Luminexa when you are starting out and want booking and invoices without a high USD subscription. One Pro plan covers the provider tools after the free trial.

When Housecall Pro may still be right

Keep Housecall Pro if you need advanced routing, larger crew tooling, or a mature FSM stack for a busy existing book of business.
