What is Luminexa?

Luminexa is a local service booking platform. Customers in Toronto find nearby providers and book open time slots. Businesses publish services, a service area, and a calendar.

Does Luminexa operate in Toronto?

Yes. Toronto is a launch city alongside Ottawa. You search by address or postal code. A provider appears only if you are inside both your search radius and their service area.

Can I book snow removal, car detailing, or gardening in Toronto?

Yes — those are jobs we highlight. Snow and garden crews often list under Yard & outdoors; detailers often list under Auto & vehicles. Availability depends on which Toronto providers are on Luminexa.

How do I book a service?

Open Luminexa, enter your Toronto address or postal code, choose a category, compare providers, and pick an open slot or send a request. You can also install the Luminexa app from Google Play.

Do you also serve Ottawa?

Yes. Ottawa is a launch city alongside Toronto. The same booking app works in both cities.
