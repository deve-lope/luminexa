Offer services

Card payments

When a customer pays an invoice by card, Luminexa takes a 0.5% platform fee. Stripe’s card processing fee is separate. Cash and e-transfer mark-as-paid still work without that platform fee.

Is Luminexa free for customers?

Yes. Finding providers, booking slots, messaging, and Gig wall posts are free for customers.

What do providers pay?

Luminexa Pro is $9.99 CAD per month after the free trial. That unlocks the provider dashboard and business tools.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. New eligible businesses get a 30-day Pro trial — no card required to start. Add a payment method before the trial ends to keep Pro.

Do you take a cut of every job?

There is no booking commission on cash or e-transfer. On card invoice payments only, Luminexa takes 0.5% plus Stripe’s own processing fee.

Where does Luminexa work?

Launch cities are Ottawa and Toronto, Ontario. Matching uses your address and each provider’s service area.

Ready to book?

Open the Luminexa homepage to search your address, compare local providers, and pick a time.
