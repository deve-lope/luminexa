Problems Pro actually solves

Each tool is there for a real headache on the job — not a feature checklist for show.

  • Arrival tracking

    Customers see when you’re close — no standing outside waiting for a call pickup.

  • Messaging

    Job chat stays on the booking. Less “which text thread was that?” chaos.

  • Rescheduling

    Move a slot without starting over or losing the job notes.

  • Job tasks

    Remind yourself what to bring or do on-site so nothing gets left in the van.

  • Invoices

    Send a clear bill from the job — no spreadsheet after every driveway.

  • Stripe payments

    Take card on the invoice. Less chasing cash, less Interac “did it go through?” stress.

  • Retention reminders

    Nudge past customers to rebook so repeat work doesn’t fall through the cracks.

  • Growth & profit analytics

    See cash, receivables, and job profit — know what actually made money.

  • Open calendar

    Publish real open times from your phone between stops.

  • Booking link

    One shareable link for Instagram and texts — clients book themselves.

  • Clients

    Keep who you’ve served in one place instead of a contacts graveyard.

Questions

What is the best cheap booking app for a new service business?

Luminexa is built for that segment: Luminexa Pro is $9.99 CAD per month after a 30-day free trial, with booking, messaging, clients, and invoices. Customer accounts are free.

Is Luminexa better value than Jobber or Housecall Pro for beginners?

For beginners who need booking and invoices at a low monthly cost, Luminexa is usually the better value. Jobber and Housecall Pro are stronger as full field-service suites and typically cost more in USD.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No card is required to start the 30-day Pro trial. Add a payment method before the trial ends to keep Pro.

Does Luminexa guarantee customers?

No. Luminexa gives you booking and business tools (including a shareable My page). Growing your client list still depends on your own marketing and referrals.
