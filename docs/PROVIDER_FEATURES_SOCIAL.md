# Luminexa — Provider features for social media

One source list of **valuable features we’ve built for service providers**. Use this for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Reels captions, carousels, and creator briefs.

**Audience:** local service business owners and crews (field + shop).  
**Pitch angle:** customers use Luminexa free; **you** run the business on **Luminexa Pro**.  
**Geography:** launch focus Ottawa & Toronto (Ontario); expanding — don’t claim Canada-wide coverage.  
**Install:** Google Play / website only. **Never** “Add to Home Screen” or browser PWA.

Related creative pack: [`INSTAGRAM_PROVIDER_REELS_BRIEF.md`](INSTAGRAM_PROVIDER_REELS_BRIEF.md)

## 30-second pitch (providers)

**Luminexa** is a local booking marketplace plus a mobile-first **provider dashboard**. Customers find you by address/ZIP and miles, see prices, and book **real open time slots**. You publish services, set your service area, run schedule / requests / jobs / messages, send invoices with **Canadian tax**, take **card pay** via Stripe, bid on **Gig wall** jobs, and keep regulars coming back with reminders, reviews, and a shareable **My page**.

**Taglines (use these):**

- *Book local services. Effortlessly.*
- *When you need it done, book local help.*

**CTA:** Offer services / Start Luminexa Pro → [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.luminexa.app) or https://app.luminex-a.com/

**Pro pricing (safe to say):** ~**$9.99 CAD / month**, yearly option, free trial (no card to start). Customers always free.

## Feature catalog (provider perspective)

### 1. Booking & requests

| Feature | What it is | Why it matters for you |
|---------|------------|------------------------|
| **Real open time slots** | Customers book (or request) times you actually opened | Stops “are you free Thursday?” text chains |
| **How customers book** | **Automatic booking**, **You approve each booking**, or **Request access first** | Match how tight you want control |
| **Requests inbox** | Booking requests + quote inquiries — accept / decline | One place for “maybes” instead of DMs |
| **Cancel cutoff** | Policy hours before the slot | Fewer last-minute no-shows with clear rules |
| **Job statuses** | Requested → quoted → confirmed → in progress → needs return → completed / cancelled | Track the job from ask to paid |

**Social angle:** *Still booking over text? Put open times up and let customers book.*

---

### 2. Rescheduling

| Feature | What it is | Why it matters for you |
|---------|------------|------------------------|
| **Provider reschedule / change time** | Move a booking to another open slot from the dashboard | Life happens — keep the job, don’t cancel |
| **Customer reschedule requests** | Customer asks to move; you get notified (in-app + push) | Handle changes without starting over from scratch |
| **Blocked customers** | Org block stops booking **and** reschedule | Protect your calendar from problem accounts |

**Social angle:** *Job moved? Change the time in the app — keep the customer, keep the slot filled.*

---

### 3. Schedule, availability & capacity

| Feature | What it is | Why it matters for you |
|---------|------------|------------------------|
| **Schedule** | See what’s booked / open on a mobile timeline | Run the day from your phone |
| **Availability** | Weekly hours or flexi dates; timezone-aware | Publish when you actually work |
| **Jobs at the same time** | How many jobs can share one window (crew size or chairs) | Don’t leave money idle if you have 2 techs / 2 bays |
| **Block unavailable time** | Mark time off on the timeline | Vacations and shop closures stay off the book |

**Social angle:** *Your crew isn’t one person — set how many jobs can run in the same slot.*

---

### 4. Services, pricing & My page

| Feature | What it is | Why it matters for you |
|---------|------------|------------------------|
| **Services catalog** | Publish what you offer | Customers know what to book before they message |
| **Pricing types** | Fixed, price range, typical price, or **quote on request** | Fit detailing packages *and* “call for estimate” work |
| **Mobile vs shop** | Field jobs vs come-to-you / chair | Clear where the work happens |
| **My page** | Shareable public page `/book/:slug` | Send one link to regulars — they book your open times |
| **Storefront branding** | Cover, logo, gallery (up to 5), optional website link | Look like a real business, not a random number |

**Social angle:** *One link. Your prices. Your open times. Clients book themselves.*

---

### 5. Service area (dual radius) & multi-location

| Feature | What it is | Why it matters for you |
|---------|------------|------------------------|
| **Service pins + travel radius** | Where you work and how far you’ll go | Only show to people you actually serve |
| **Dual radius** | Customer search miles **and** your radius must both match | Less wasted drive time / fewer “too far” jobs |
| **Multiple locations** | Up to 20 pins on one profile (or register a separate business) | Cover two yards / two shops without fake addresses |

**Social angle:** *Only show up where you actually serve.*

---

### 6. Android & iOS apps + notifications

| Feature | What it is | Why it matters for you |
|---------|------------|------------------------|
| **Android app** | Live on Google Play (`com.luminexa.app`) | Run the business from your pocket |
| **iOS app** | Same product via Capacitor; App Store when listing is live | Phone + web; max **2 devices** signed in at once |
| **Push notifications** | New bookings, reschedules, messages, gigs, reminders, billing | Don’t miss a request while you’re on a job |
| **In-app Notifications** | Activity center for booking actions, gigs, subscription, etc. | Catch up when you’re back in the van |
| **Web dashboard** | Same account on browser | Desk + phone without juggling tools |

**Install rule for posts:** Play Store or app.luminex-a.com — **never** Chrome/Safari “Install app” / Add to Home Screen.

**Social angle:** *New booking? It pings your phone — not buried in texts.*

### 7. Reminders (keep customers showing up)

| Feature | What it is | Why it matters for you |
|---------|------------|------------------------|
| **Appointment reminders** | Email + in-app + **push** before the job | Fewer no-shows; customers feel taken care of |
| **Invoice follow-ups** | Email nudges on unpaid invoices | Get paid without awkward chase calls |
| **Task reminders** | Nudges for incomplete prep tasks | Show up prepared — better reviews |

**Do not claim SMS** in ads — reminders are email, in-app, and push (not text messages).

**Social angle:** *We remind them so you don’t have to.*

---

### 8. Messaging

| Feature | What it is | Why it matters for you |
|---------|------------|------------------------|
| **Messages** | Chat with customers in the app | Keep the thread with the job, not lost in SMS |
| **Photo attachments** | Send / receive pictures in chat | Confirm damage, driveway, color — before you roll |
| **Report & Block** | Safety tools from the chat menu | Cut off harassment without nuking your whole day |
| **Clients block (booking)** | Separate from chat block — stops booking / reschedule | Protect calendar from banned customers |

**Social angle:** *Photos in chat. No more “what’s the address again?” chaos.*

---

### 9. Gig wall (extra demand beyond your catalog)

| Feature | What it is | Why it matters for you |
|---------|------------|------------------------|
| **Gig wall** | Customers pin custom help requests nearby | Jobs that don’t fit a fixed price list |
| **Quotes / bids** | Price, what’s covered, optional duration | You choose what fits your crew |
| **Counters** | Customer can counter; you accept / decline | Negotiate without phone tag |
| **Privacy-safe board** | No public street address / phone / email on the open wall | Contact after a quote is accepted |
| **My quotes** | Track what you’ve bid on | Follow up winners into real bookings |

**Social angle:** *They pin the job. You place the bid. Extra work in your area.*

---

### 10. Stripe payments & getting paid

| Feature | What it is | Why it matters for you |
|---------|------------|------------------------|
| **Stripe Connect** | Connect your payout account in Billing | Take card pay without a separate merchant stack |
| **Card pay on invoices** | Customer pays invoice online (Checkout) | Less “I’ll e-transfer later” |
| **Platform + Stripe fees** | Small platform fee + Stripe’s fee (be honest if asked) | Don’t invent “zero fee forever” |
| **Instant Payout** | Cash out Connect balance when available | Get money to the bank faster after busy days |

**Social angle:** *Send the invoice. They pay by card. Done.*

---

### 11. Invoices & Canadian tax (bill generation)

| Feature | What it is | Why it matters for you |
|---------|------------|------------------------|
| **Invoice generation** | Issue when completing jobs; PDF for records | Look legit; keep books clean |
| **Canadian tax lines** | Tax from **your business province** — not a guess | Correct GST / HST / PST / QST / RST lines |
| **Tax by province (examples)** | ON/Atlantic → **HST**; AB → **GST**; BC/SK → **GST+PST**; QC → **GST+QST**; MB → **GST+RST** | Built for Canadian service businesses |
| **Subtotal, discount, total** | Referral coupon credit applies **pre-tax** | Transparent bills customers trust |
| **Currency** | CAD by default | Matches how you price locally |

**Social angle:** *Invoices with the right Canadian tax — not a spreadsheet scramble at month-end.*

## Do / don’t for social posts

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Sell Pro + nearby bookings + Gig wall + ops dashboard | Promise income, #1 ranking, or “guaranteed jobs” |
| Say customers are free; Pro ~$9.99 CAD/mo + trial | Say the business side is free forever |
| Name real features (Jobs at the same time, Gig wall, My page) | Invent AI matching, SMS reminders, or public phone numbers on Gig wall |
| CTA → Play Store / app.luminex-a.com | “Add to Home Screen” / Chrome install / browser PWA |
| Ottawa & Toronto as launch cities | Claim Canada-wide coverage |
| Say Canadian tax from business province | Invent a specific GST/HST “registration number” feature |
| Say Android live; iOS when App Store is live | Claim iOS is live if the listing isn’t public yet |
