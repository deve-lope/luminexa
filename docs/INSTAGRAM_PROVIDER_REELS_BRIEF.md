## 2. What Luminexa is (provider pitch)

**Luminexa** is a local service booking marketplace plus a mobile-first **provider dashboard**.

Customers find nearby providers by address/ZIP and miles, see prices, and book **real open time slots**. Businesses publish services, a service area, and a calendar; manage schedule, requests, jobs, messages, and invoices under **Luminexa Pro**.

**Taglines (use these, don’t invent new brand lines):**

- *Book local services. Effortlessly.*
- *When you need it done, book local help.*

**Store / site**

- Web: https://app.luminex-a.com/
- Android: Google Play (`com.luminexa.app`)
- iOS: App Store when live (same product)

**Never** tell viewers to “Install app” from Chrome/Safari, “Add to Home Screen,” or install a browser PWA. Install path is the **store listing** (or the website for providers signing up).

## 4. Gig wall (customers post → providers bid)

**Gig wall** is a community notice board (“help requests pinned for providers nearby”).

**Flow (accurate):**

1. A **customer** posts a request: title, description, category, city/postal, search radius, up to **2 photos**.
2. Public posts stay privacy-safe — **no** street address, phone, or email on the open board. Providers contact privately **after a quote is accepted**.
3. **Providers** in dual-radius of their service area see relevant gigs, comment, and place a **quote** (price, what’s covered, optional duration). One quote per business per post; customer can counter.
4. Customer accepts → path toward a real booking (schedule an open slot).
5. Posts expire (~30 days).

**How to pitch Gig wall to providers in Reels:**

- Extra jobs beyond your fixed service list — custom help requests in your area.
- Bid / quote on pins near you; win the ones that fit your crew.
- Demand comes to you; you don’t chase cold DMs with public phone numbers.

**Customer angle only as demand:** “Customers pin what they need. You place a bid.” Do not make customer-primary Reels from this brief.

## 5. Creative constraints (for AI video + human shoot)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Format | Vertical **9:16**; primary length **15 seconds** (can note a 30s cut if asked) |
| Hook | First **0–3s** must stop the scroll — pain or curiosity, not logo-first |
| Body | **3–12s** — show phone UI and/or real job vibe (driveway snow, detailing bay, garden, van) |
| Close | **12–15s** — clear CTA + brand name **Luminexa** |
| POV | Second person: *you* run the business |
| Visuals | Prefer real work + app screen recordings; avoid purple SaaS glow clichés |
| Verticals to feature | Snow removal, car detailing, gardening (launch favorites); cleaning/handyman OK |
| Geography | Ottawa / Toronto OK to name; don’t claim national coverage |
| Pricing | OK: Pro ~$9.99 CAD/mo, free trial, customers free. Don’t invent other fees or “unlimited free forever for businesses.” |
| Install | Play Store / app site only — **never** Add to Home Screen / browser PWA |
| Claims | Don’t invent features (no AI matching, no guaranteed income, no public phone on Gig wall) |
| Tone | Direct, local, practical — not “disrupt the industry” |

**On-screen text:** short, large, readable on mute. **Captions:** benefit + CTA + soft hashtags (local + trade).
